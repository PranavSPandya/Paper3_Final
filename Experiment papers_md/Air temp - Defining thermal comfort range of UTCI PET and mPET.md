Discussion
• Further investigation of the thermal comfort ranges in
Taiwan and Europe showed that for the PET, the lowest
acceptable (-2) temperature in Taiwan was 10°C higher
than that in Europe, indicating that residents of
subtropical regions are less tolerant of low
temperatures.
• For the UTCI, the thermal comfort ranges
recommended worldwide differed considerably from
the Taiwanese ranges derived from local questionnaire
data. This result implies that the thermal comfort range
of thermal comfort indices should be based on the
results obtained from local questionnaires to meet the
local’s needs.
