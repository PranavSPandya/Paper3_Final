peratures, reducing their thermal tolerance. In environments with broadcasts or conversations, respondents
exhibit slightly higher thermal tolerance compared to the average neutral temperature. Finally, based on
meteorological characteristics at different survey points and respondent feedback, optimization strategies for
design are proposed.
