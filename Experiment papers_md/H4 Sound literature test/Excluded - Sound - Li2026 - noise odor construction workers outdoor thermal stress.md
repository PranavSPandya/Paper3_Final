2.3.4. Thermal stress category
Outdoor thermal stress was quantified using the PET, a thermophy-
siological index derived from the Munich Energy Balance Model for
Individuals (MEMI) that integrates all major heat exchange processes to
express complex outdoor thermal conditions in terms of equivalent in-
door reference temperatures [54,55]. Meteorological parameters (Ta,
RH, Va, and Tmrt) together with participant-specific anthropometric and
physiological attributes (stature, body mass, age, sex, clothing insu-
lation, and metabolic rate) were input into the RayMan model to derive
PET values for each experimental condition.
Subsequent analyses were performed using Microsoft Excel and
Origin 2018. Linear regression analysis was conducted to establish the
relationship between PET and aggregated mean thermal sensation votes
(MTSV) across 1 ◦C PET intervals. Based on established thermal
perception thresholds [56], experimental conditions were stratified into
three distinct thermal stress categories: 17.36 ◦C ≤ PET < 32.43 ◦C
