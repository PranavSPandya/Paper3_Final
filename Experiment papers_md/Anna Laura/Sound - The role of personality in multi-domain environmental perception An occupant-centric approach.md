pants showing stronger appreciation for natural ones. Overall, the findings demonstrate that occupant perception
cannot be understood through single-domain analyses alone. Integrating cross-domain interactions and
personality-driven variability into adaptive comfort models and occupant-centric design strategies is essential to
support buildings that are both energy-efficient and responsive to human diversity.
