Step 2: Physical Sensor Fusion (all environmental columns audited; 33 active continuous candidates)

The 93 environmental columns consist of 53 raw sensor hardware channels (UCM microclimate, LYS photopic, Atmotube particle, microphone) and 40 derived/calculated indices (bioclimatic UTCI/PET/MRT, computer vision GVI segmentation, deep learning YAMNet event proportions, astronomical clearness). Before statistical modeling, 55 raw, duplicate, or unilateral channels are consolidated via 6 physical measurement rules:

  • 1. Bilateral Air Pollution Fusion: Left and right Atmotubes are averaged (atmo_pm1_avg, atmo_pm2.5_avg, atmo_pm10_avg) to eliminate heading-dependent body-shielding.
  • 2. Photopic Lighting Harmonization: Raw RGB bands and discrete step categories (lux_cat) are excluded in favor of continuous physical lux (lys_lux_avg).
  • 3. Radiant Plane & Mast Consolidation: 10 directional IR spot angles, pyranometer flux, and multi-tier temperatures are synthesized by standard biometeorology into composite Mean Radiant Temperature (ucm_MRT) and reference ambient temperature (ucm_AIR_temp).
  • 4. Removal of Solar Duplicates: Solar-forced duplicate formulations (ucm_UTCI_SR, ucm_PET_SR, ucm_MRT_S) and celestial geometry are excluded in favor of standard ISO/COST formulations.
  • 5. Acoustic Event Filtering: Unclassified audio residue and categorical string labels are removed; physical LAeq (ucm_SND_dBA) and continuous YAMNet event proportions are retained.
  • 6. Protocol Flag Removal: Discrete indoor/outdoor flag (ucm_IO_flag) is excluded since trial boundaries are controlled by protocol.
