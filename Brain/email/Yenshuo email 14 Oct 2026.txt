I just checked the resubmission. There are a lot of errors I flagged that haven't been fixed, and I also found new problems.
•	Highlights and cover letter still claim a shade effect. The highlight “Shade sustains biking under heat stress in spring and summer” and the cover letter’s “shade sustains biking on hotter days” contradict §3.1.6 and Table 1 (Q2: 0 shaded observations; Q3: 5). The Conclusion also contradicts itself: it says “A robust mitigating shade effect was not established,” then recommends that “shading along high-use corridors can mitigate heat-stress effects.”
•	The abstract mislabels H1. “Rain and wind conditions suppress biking by (−7.40% to −15.38%)” reports the rain-only (H1) range. The abstract also presents non-significant ranges (H2 Q3, H5, H7) as if they were effects.
•	Section 3.3 still contains the old RMSE values. It gives city baseline 91.57, M4 64.94, M5 65.71 and M6 60.96 bikes/h. The new Table 3 gives 80.96, 53.16, 55.08 and 53.52.
•	Section 3.3.1 still uses the old station R² values. It cites CB2105 0.61, CJE181 0.65, CJM90 0.57, CAT17 0.69 and CB1101 0.80. Yet Section 3.3 says CJE181 has R² = −3.97. Section 3.3.1 also places CJE181 in Cluster 1, but your cluster list puts it in Cluster 2.
•	Section 4.2.1 and 4.2.2 contradict the new results. 
o	§4.2.2 claims “a 2.99 bikes/hour RMSE reduction attributable to micro-meteorology.” The new temporal gain is 1.99 bikes/h, and for the new-station model micro-meteorology increased RMSE (+0.36).
o	The §4.2.1 paragraph beginning “The RMSE reduction observed in the new station count prediction model…” explains a gain that no longer exists.
•	Temporal model R² in the text does not match Table 2. The text gives Model 2 R² = 0.87. Table 2’s average for Model 2 is 0.86.
•	The gap definition contradicts itself in four places. 
o	Section 2.5: imputation is for periods “< 6 hours.”
o	Section 2.5.1: outages are “more than six / at least 6” consecutive zeros.
o	Section 3.2: gaps are 0 counts for “more than 6 consecutive hours.”
o	Section4.2: imputation covers “prolonged periods,” and CB1142 had 100% zeros in August.
•	The winter-GVI sensitivity contradicts Figure 4. Section 3.1.8 says only CJM90 flips. Figure 4 shows CVT387 also flips (summer 6.92% No-Green, winter 16.07% Green). Winter GVI exceeding summer GVI there is also suspicious and may be a segmentation artefact.
•	The decision tree (Fig. 7) appears to misassign CEK31. CEK31 has summer GVI 55.15%, which is above 50%. That sends it to Cluster 2, or to Cluster 3/4 depending on density and distance. Your list puts it in Cluster 1.
•	The UTCI thresholds are inconsistent. 
o	Introduction and H3: strong heat UTCI ≥ 32, moderate 26 ≤ UTCI < 32.
o	Section 2.4: strong heat > 32, moderate 26 < UTCI ≤ 32.
•	GVI capture date is given three ways: May 2025 (Section 2.3.2), June 2025 (Fig. 2), and May/June (Section 4.3.1). Pick one.
•	H6 cannot be tested as designed. Under strong heat stress, Tmrt (and therefore UTCI) is driven by direct sun, so Shade = 1 and Hot = 1 almost never occur together. This explains the 0 and 5 observations.
•	The H7 estimate does not test the H7 hypothesis. H7’s N values (71 + 162 = 233) equal the Q2 hot hours. The reported IRR is therefore green vs no-green within hot hours, not the Green×Hot interaction the hypothesis describes.
•	“28 planned comparisons” seems to be wrong. Only about 18 contrasts were estimated, and H6 Q3 has no Holm p-value.
•	The new-station prediction formula has a unit error. “Volume profile (hourly count ÷ station mean) × Cluster Mean Annual Count” gives the wrong units. It should be the cluster mean hourly count.
•	Model 4 and the “20 indicators” are described inconsistently. 
o	The Model 4 text lists Day of Year, but §2.5.2 says it is excluded.
o	Model 5’s text lists Shade, but Eq. (8) removed it.
o	Eq. (9) uses Wind_t instead of Wind_i,t.
o	The “20 indicators” include the target (Bike Count).
•	Temporal Model 1 has no hour term (Eq. 4). If you fit one model per station and hour, say so explicitly. Day of Year as a linear term together with Month is also collinear and not cyclic.
•	Rain threshold of 0.01 mm/h. Typical gauge resolution is 0.1 mm.
•	Sensitivity analyses are announced but not reported. §2.6 promises continuous-exposure and threshold-grid results, but only the winter-GVI check appears. The same applies to “block-masking 3–72 h” and “excluding imputed records” in §3.2.
•	Evaluation data include outage zeros. The LOSO evaluation uses “available station-hour rows,” yet §3.3.1 attributes errors to outage zeros at CVT387 and CB1142.
•	Discussion claims are not supported by the data. 
o	“Warmer conditions correspond to increased biking” (§4.1; struck in the Conclusion but still in §4.1).
o	“Cold–rain conditions” were never tested jointly.
o	“Strongest declines … during typical peak hours” cannot be tested, because the analysis uses only weekend hours 9–16.
o	Comparing cold and heat effects across quarters with different references deserves a caveat.

These are the major problems I notice so far and there are much more minor issues. I suggest you to start correcting asap to prevent the last-minute rush again.
