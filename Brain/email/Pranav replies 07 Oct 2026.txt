Regarding empatica__met, it is obtained directly from the Empatica cloud Biomarkers export and was not recalculated in our processing pipeline. The raw AVRO files do not contain this MET biomarker.
 
The study-derived stress composite (eyetracker_stress_composite) combines Pupil Labs Neon fixation count (FC) and fixation duration (FD). These are min-max scaled per participant across all phases, with fixation duration inverted so that higher values in both components indicate higher visual stress: FC_scaled = (FC - min FC) / (max FC - min FC), FD_scaled = 1 - [(FD - min FD) / (max FD - min FD)], and Stress Composite = (FC_scaled + FD_scaled) / 2.

The Clearness Index was calculated as: k_t(t) = G_H(t) / G_0(t)
where G_H is ucm_SUN_Gh, the measured global horizontal irradiance, and:
G_0(t) = G_sc × E_0(n) × sin(α(t))
with G_sc = 1367 W/m², E_0(n) = 1 + 0.033 × cos(2πn/365), and α(t) taken from ucm_SUN_alt and converted from degrees to radians.
The formula reference for the clearness-index method described by https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/17/10/2328

Regarding sound classification, following the two Bluyssen papers, I treated sound level, frequency characteristics, and sound type as separate acoustic characteristics. For the frequency component, I implemented a frequency-analysis pipeline including 5 broad-band energy proportions (20–125 Hz, 125–1000 Hz, 1000–4000 Hz, 4000–8000 Hz, and 8000–20000 Hz), 9 octave-band relative-energy descriptors centred at 31.5, 63, 125, 250, 500, 1000, 2000, 4000, and 8000 Hz.
