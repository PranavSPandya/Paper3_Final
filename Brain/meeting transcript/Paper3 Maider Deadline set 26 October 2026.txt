## 4. Proposed scientific story

I would test three possible contributions before deciding the final narrative.

Contribution A — Cycling and walking produce different physiological responses under different urban microclimates. This is potentially your strongest novelty. But you must separate physical exertion, wind exposure and thermal conditions. Do not assume a difference in skin temperature means cycling is universally more thermally comfortable.

Contribution B — Perceived comfort and physiological responses are only partly concordant. H2 could be valuable if you demonstrate which associations are present, their uncertainty and why other indicators may respond on different timescales. You cannot claim short phase duration explains nonsignificant findings without supporting evidence.

Contribution C — Sound and thermal stress did not show a statistically supported interaction under the observed exposure conditions. This is a legitimate finding, but the explanation must come from the measured sound distribution, precision of the interaction estimate and comparisons with previous studies. A minimum above 40 dBA alone does not establish homogeneous exposure.

The paper's novelty should be based primarily on A and B unless C or the GVI/SVF analysis reveals a stronger, robust contribution.
