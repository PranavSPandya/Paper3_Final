# H4 sound evidence: what the papers tested and what we actually fitted

Updated 7 November 2026. **This is the one active H4 methods guide.** It replaces the three older screening/replacement/discrepancy documents, now retained under `documents/_archive/H4_methods_20261107/`. Original papers are evidence; this guide records their mapping to our data. H5 is now the combined-exposure analysis, not the retired grid analysis.

| Participant × phase cells | 44 | 55 |

Sound and temperature vary: the core phase-energy-mean dBA ranges from **55.86 to 81.15 dB(A)** and phase-mean UTCI from **20.61 to 35.71 °C**. Sound/UTCI have 2,112 finite core ten-second observations; adding Tram gives 2,629 finite observations of each. Individual physiological models have their own missingness and sample counts.

### 1.1 Four sound representations, where supported by the method

H4 does **not** use only a majority cut. The four representations are attempted in the compatible measured-sound blocks, using the same 10 s / 8-min data. Some fits are not estimable; vote-to-vote, thermal-only and site-specific blocks do not use every representation. Corridor represents environmental context, not an isolated sound exposure. Report these analyses separately.

Papers and how the four encodings enter:

- **Moshammer, Biel, Kraus, Rodrigues:** substitute the representation for sound level in the explicitly adapted source-inspired model. Jin uses two ANOVA levels when the encoding is binary, otherwise quartiles.
- **Korpilo:** source run keeps the NDSI analogue; the same LMM is also fitted with each of the four encodings in place of NDSI.
- **Pigliautile:** paired traffic-minus-nature person means for encodings 2–3, and thermal-stratum person-mean Spearman for encodings 1 and 4. Full-session vote–sound Spearman is fitted once under Chiucchiù, not repeated under a second family.
- **Chiucchiù:** paired physiological warmer/cooler means; Table 5 analogue for encodings 2–3; full-session person-mean Spearman of thermal/pleasantness/acoustic votes against encodings 1 and 4. The source TSV paired test is not fitted because TSV is absent; TC warmer/cooler is already in Pigliautile.
- **Chen:** `rating ~ zUTCI * zSound + (1\|participant)` for each encoding. Primary dBA also includes `zNaturalShare`.
- **Qin:** `TC ~ zSound * zRH` on all four encodings (humidity, not UTCI).
- **Gao:** `PRS ~ zUTCI * zSound` on all four encodings; two-way ANOVA of PRS on dBA × UTCI quartiles.
- **Wu:** `OC ~ zUTCI + zSound` on all four encodings; `TC ~ I(dBA≥60) * zUTCI`; median-split analogue on encodings 2–4.
- **Xu:** linear `AC ~ zSound + (1|participant)` sensitivity; combined `TC ~ I(air_temp_c>32) * I(dBA>65) + (1|participant)` only when all four conditions are observed. These are air-temperature thresholds, not UTCI. The ASV 64.8-dB knot is not imposed on AC.
- **Zhen:** unstandardized `TC ~ UTCI * dBA` sensitivity without Chen’s natural-share covariate. Alternative encodings are not refitted because they reparameterize Chen TC. TSV/NUTCI remain unavailable.
- **Mohammadzadeh:** `AC ~ zUTCI`, `AC ~ TC`, and the primary dBA interaction without Chen’s natural-share covariate. Alternative-encoding AC interactions are canonical Chen fits, not duplicated.
- **Cureau:** no sound exposure (vote vs vote by phase). **Zhang SciRep:** perceived AC × UTCI, not a measured encoding. **Zhang BSim:** Spearman vs dBA by phase. **Song, Ueberham, Li 2025, Jin 2020 chamber:** not fitted.

Fifteen spectral descriptors remain an extra substitution on Moshammer/Jin/Biel/Rodrigues/Kraus only. They are not one of the four encodings.

Walking is the more stable loudness pair (energy-mean **64.4 dB(A)** WalkG vs **69.4** WalkU). Cycling is louder and more variable (**65.8** BikeG vs **76.3** BikeU). Encoding 3 seconds: of 2,111 core 10 s rows, WalkG is 28% vehicle>rural, BikeG 25%, BikeU 54%, WalkU **34% vehicle / 66% nature**. The previously cited native 1-s output path is absent at this audit; see the measurement-provenance limit below. Nature versus traffic **inside the same thermal bin** stays sparse, because urban bouts are almost always the warmer ones (cooler: 21 green + 2 urban; warmer: 1 green + 20 urban). That does not remove the 11-person corridor contrast.

These observations permit model fitting. **Kraus absolute LF/HF mapping was corrected and rerun in the subsequent correctness audit below.** They do not turn 11 people into 44 or 55 independent people. Tram adds another repeated condition, not new participants. The headphone experiment still cannot be reconstructed: temperature and route travel together. Short recordings also prevent several source lag/window analyses. Statistical significance is not a data-sufficiency certificate.

## 2. What does “discomfort” mean here?

| Construct | Exact merged column | Recorded meaning | What it does **not** measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thermal comfort | `questionnaire_thermal_comfort_1_5` | Answer to “Please describe your comfort [Thermal]” | Not hot/cold thermal sensation; not a physiological stress score |
| Acoustic comfort | `questionnaire_acoustic_comfort_1_5` | Answer to “Please describe your comfort [Acoustic (Urban Noise)]” | Not measured dBA and not a subjective loudness/intensity vote |
| Overall/current comfort | `questionnaire_current_comfort_1_7` | Answer to “How comfortable do you feel right now?” | Not acoustic-specific or thermal-specific |
| Physiological response | HR, HRV, tonic EDA, wrist skin temperature | Measured bodily response; outcome and transformation depend on the paper block | Not a direct report of comfort, and not an isolated sound-induced stress measure |

The thermal/acoustic scale is exactly: **1 extremely uncomfortable; 2 very uncomfortable; 3 non comfortable; 4 slightly non comfortable; 5 comfortable.** It is not a bipolar five-point scale with “neutral” at 3. Overall comfort runs from 1 to 7; higher means more comfortable according to the questionnaire framework.

The script models the original comfort scores. It does **not** construct a separate discomfort variable. For a regression on thermal comfort, a negative coefficient means lower reported thermal comfort. An omnibus F-test only establishes some difference between groups; it does not establish a decreasing trend or that higher dBA causes discomfort. Higher skin temperature or altered HRV must be reported as that physiological association, not renamed “discomfort.”

Audit: all **55 thermal + 55 acoustic + 55 overall** phase ratings matched their raw responses and scoring, using participant and within-participant response order. Raw source: `D:/Pranav/docker/Paper3_Github/rawdata/05_questionnaire/Recurring questionnaire.csv`; scoring map: `D:/Pranav/docker/ExpData/scripts/05_questionnaire_build.py`. This checks scores/response identity; it is not a fresh independent audit of phase-schedule assignment. The current analysis input remains the Desktop project's merged CSV.

**No entire source study is replicated exactly.** Some statistical procedures match, but our design/measurements differ. This is not a reason to abandon the data; it determines the claim and the adaptations that must be reported. The direct question “does sound worsen perceived thermal comfort?” uses **TC as outcome**. A physiology model answers a different question.

## 5. Sound spectrum and source substitutions

Section 1.1 lists the sound/context representations attempted in compatible blocks; it does not imply every source method uses all four. The eight YAMNet class proportions are encoding 4 (continuous shares, not a majority cut). Fifteen spectral descriptors remain an extra substitution on Moshammer/Jin/Biel/Rodrigues/Kraus only.

These substitutions are **our extensions**, not methods those papers performed with these 23 variables. Frequency-band power fractions are not octave-band dBA levels. A YAMNet nature proportion is not calibrated sound pressure or independent proof of birdsong. Both may contain information beyond overall dBA, but a frequency range alone cannot identify a biological sound source.

For alternatives, physical dB cutoffs are replaced explicitly: Jin uses descriptor quartiles; Kraus uses a descriptor median knot and one-SD slope; Biel reports one-IQR slopes; Rodrigues standardizes max/SD summaries. These units and cutoffs must be stated. Their substitution results are not literal replications of a source dBA equation.

## 6. How to read significance and claims

- Keep **p_value** for the test and **q_value** for our existing Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment. `FDR_significant` means q<.05. Correction is within source paper × test × sound descriptor, **not across all descriptors/models**. State the family in Methods; do not count repeated rows as independent discoveries.
- Source decisions differ: Rodrigues has its rounded-p/absolute-beta rule; Korpilo selected at .10; Pigliautile's comparison was descriptive. “Paper rule” is not universally p<.05. Existing generic labels must be read with this mapping.
- A significant noise main effect does not establish heat×sound interaction. A significant temperature block is not evidence for sound. A significant warmer/cooler comparison is not evidence for nature/traffic. The four encodings in §1.1 are different hypotheses; do not pool them. A class-proportion slope is not a majority contrast and is not calibrated dBA.
- Importantly, **Jin's original thermal-comfort analysis found no UTCI×LAeq interaction in any season** (Table 5), although LAeq main effects were significant. Not every published paper establishes the interaction we might hope to find.
- Eleven people can support limited, explicitly uncertain paired/repeated-measures analyses. No universal subject-count threshold, successful fit, high dBA, extra Tram phase or robustness test guarantees publication readiness. Use the model-matched robustness checks; report estimates/intervals, participant counts, dependence handling and failed fits. Do not select the model because it passes FDR.

### 6.1 Equivalent models and correction families

Paper labels do not define independent statistical evidence. For alternative encodings, `TC ~ UTCI * sound + (1|participant)` (Zhen adaptation) and `TC ~ zUTCI * zSound + (1|participant)` (Chen adaptation) have the same fitted model under an invertible centering/scaling transformation, on the same observations. Coefficient units and main-effect reference points change; fitted values and the interaction hypothesis do not. This equivalence does **not** apply to Chen's primary dBA specification with its additional natural-share covariate, nor to Zhen's remaining unstandardized dBA interaction (no nature-share term). The alternative-encoding Mohammadzadeh AC interaction likewise duplicated Chen's AC interaction specification.

Those duplicated encoding rows are **no longer fitted**. A previous catalogue run had the same corridor×UTCI interaction on thermal comfort at p≈0.01756 under both Chen and Zhen labels; Chen q≈0.05268 (three outcomes in its family) versus Zhen q≈0.01756 (one outcome). Those were not contradictory findings. A one-test BH family leaves p unchanged. Do not select a paper label because it gives the smaller q, or count duplicated associations as corroboration. For remaining rows, define and justify the family for the scientific question; the exported correction is still BH within paper × test × sound variable.


The full-session Pigliautile/Chiucchiù vote–sound correlations are also fitted only once, under Chiucchiù (whose source actually used Spearman). Pigliautile keeps its distinct thermal-stratum questions. The duplicate TC warmer/cooler row under Chiucchiù is omitted; the source TSV measure is absent and the TC contrast already appears under Pigliautile. Canonical choices follow question/method identity, not which label gives the smaller q.

## 10. Historical correctness-audit snapshot — 7 November 2026, before the Scopus additions

- Kraus now uses absolute LF/HF powers, not normalized LFnu/HFnu. Its source 15-min lags remain unavailable.
- Jin now includes participant fixed intercepts. This is a necessary adaptation to repeated participants, not the source independent-respondent design. F tests retain the model residual assumptions; the added block is not an arbitrary-covariance or CR2 guarantee. Continuous within-bout binary occupancy is quantiled, not treated as dozens of factor levels.
- Spearman tests use one pair of means per participant (also within each thermal stratum), avoiding independent-treatment of repeated phase ratings. They estimate BETWEEN-person associations, a different estimand from the old pooled-phase correlations. They are not within-person sound-effect tests.
- Biel repeated windows use continuous-time AR(1) with actual elapsed 2-min units and real between-bout gaps. The random-slope and 30-min source differences remain.

## 11. Differences from the selected source studies and candidate contributions

These bullets identify differences relevant to the selected analysis blocks, not exhaustive summaries of each paper or proof of a gap across the entire field. A different measurement is not automatically a novel contribution or a valid substitute. In particular, different paper labels and standardized versus unstandardized versions do not create independent tests (§6.1).

- **Moshammer 2019:** Studied air pollution as well as noise and cardiovascular/respiratory outcomes; it was not limited to Leq and air temperature. Our selected noise-model adaptation adds source/context representations not used in that selected block.
- **Jin, Jin & Kang 2020 (squares):** LAeq 5 dB bands × UTCI stress categories on standing/sitting respondents. No wearable physiology, no YAMNet/source classifier, no within-person repeated mobility, no humidity×sound term.
- **Jin, Jin & Kang 2020 (chamber):** Assigned headphone files (birdsong, traffic, conversation, dog, dance music) at 0/20/30 °C. Not fitted here. Still no field classifier and no personal dBA on a walked route.
- **Biel 2020:** Studied air pollutants, noise and cardiovascular outcomes in daily activity. Our noise-only adaptation and source-class substitutions are extensions, not a complete reproduction of its pollutant/noise models.
- **Rodrigues 2026:** Mapped Lden and station weather, additive blocks, no interaction, no classifier.
- **Kraus 2013:** Piecewise Leq at 65 dB on 5 min HRV in daily life. No thermal-comfort votes, no source classification.
- **Korpilo 2024:** NDSI from 1–2 vs 2–8 kHz band energy, not a sound-event classifier. Frequency ratio ≠ YAMNet vehicle vs rural_natural.
- **Pigliautile 2021 / Chiucchiù 2026:** Headphone woodland vs street at two room temperatures. No field dBA, no YAMNet, no mobility.
- **Cureau 2022:** The selected Fisher tests concern comfort-domain associations; the source also used other analyses, including multinomial models. Our Fisher block is not a measured dBA effect test.
- **Chen 2023:** Respondent ranking of dominant sources at stops, then RM-ANOVA. Ranking is not YAMNet and not eight class proportions; the source ranking is not equivalent to classifier-derived proportions.
- **Ueberham 2019:** Subjective loudness/exposure intensity (not collected here). No YAMNet.
- **Li, Li & Wang 2025:** Arrival vs departure + subjective loudness. Not fitted. Mobile ASL is not a classifier.
- **Qin 2024:** LAeq bins × RH grades on TSV/TCV. No source classifier, no YAMNet, no wearable physiology, no repeated within-person mobility design.
- **Mohammadzadeh 2023:** Thermal votes → acoustic quality/loudness. No YAMNet. Plant-community labels are not sound-event classes. No wearable physiology.
- **Gao & Zhu 2025:** ROS vs UTCI/LAeq; LAeq in 5 dB bins. No classifier. Restoration scale is ROS, not PRS-11. No wearable physiology.
- **Wu 2025:** Field-noted natural/human/artificial sound types + LAeq. Those labels are site notes, not YAMNet. No wearable physiology, no class proportions.
- **Xu 2026:** Piecewise/CART/SHAP on 975 plaza questionnaires. No classifier, no YAMNet, no within-person repeated bouts, no wearable physiology.
- **Zhen 2025:** Perceived positive/neutral/negative source type (respondent report). Not YAMNet. TSV–UTCI slopes, no wearable physiology.
- **Zhang, Uchiyama & Sato 2026:** Perceived acoustic comfort and aesthetic satisfaction; PET. Its acoustic models also included measured LAeq and dominant sound type. The vote×thermal interaction selected for our adaptation must not be taken as a complete account of the source models.
- **Zhang et al. 2026 (Building Simulation):** Perceived source classed positive/neutral/negative; ENVI-met/CadnaA. Perception ranking is not YAMNet. No wearable physiology.

**Candidate contribution within this selected literature:** our pipeline combines recorded A-weighted sound summaries, classifier-derived source prevalences, spectral descriptors, physiological responses and comfort ratings during walking/cycling. The reviewed method mappings do not identify the same combination in these selected papers. This is a scoped comparison, **not a systematic demonstration of field-wide novelty**. Classifier labels require validation; corridor contrasts change several environmental conditions together; and frequency-band energy is not sound-source recognition. The contribution must be supported by reliable results and an appropriate literature search, not by the number of encodings or paper-labelled tests. Calibration and native LAeq provenance remain unresolved. A vehicle-versus-rural comparison is our derived representation, not an independently validated contrast from the source studies.
