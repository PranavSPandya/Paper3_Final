# H1–H5 screened papers
PDFs in `Experiment papers/H1 to H5` unless noted. H1–H3/H5: this study’s models + BH. H4: updated hypothesis (`H4_report.md`); European source-informed ports + local Spearman / `thermal_comfort ~ dBA_z`; nominal *p*, no BH.
## 1. H1 — better physiological and perceived comfort in green than impervious
Local: REML mixed model, participant intercept, green vs impervious, BH. Song 2013/2014 test that contrast (paired *t*/Wilcoxon). Korkmaz = reliability only. Rodrigues in §4.
**1.1 Song 2013.** *J Physiol Anthropol* 32:18. Kashiwanoha vs city, 13 men, winter 15-min walks. Paired *t* HR/HRV; Wilcoxon SD, POMS, STAI. One-sided *p*<0.05. `H1 - Song2013 - winter urban park vs city walk.pdf`
**1.2 Song 2014.** *J Physiol Anthropol* 33:8. Same contrast, 17 men, May. Same tests as 1.1. `H1 - Song2014 - spring urban park vs city walk.pdf`
**1.3 Korkmaz 2025.** *Sci Rep* 15:28940. Cologne, 26 people, same walk twice. Test–retest *r* HR/HRV/EDA (PC#1 *r*=0.60). Not an H1 contrast. `H1 - Korkmaz2025 - urban walk test-retest ambulatory physiology.pdf`
## 2. H2 — physiological responses associated with reported experiences
Local: rmcorr on four phase means (18 votes × 26 physiology), one BH family.
**2.1 Bakdash & Marusich 2017.** *Front Psychol* 8:456. Common within-person slope. Local: vote_ij = α_i + β physiology_ij + ε_ij. `H2 - Bakdash Marusich 2017 - Repeated measures correlation.pdf`
## 3. H3 — psychophysiological comfort more compromised cycling than walking
Local: same mixed model as H1; WalkU−BikeU, WalkG−BikeG. No cited paper tests this. Cnaan/Lenth/BH in §6.
**3.1 Rupi & Krizek 2019.** *Sustainability* 11(21):6089. Bologna, 13 cyclists, gaze at intersections. Eye-tracking citation, not walk–cycle comfort. `H3 - Rupi Krizek 2019 - visual eye gaze cycling urban intersections.pdf`
## 4. H4 — The presence of noise will increase thermal discomfort
**Updated H4 only** (see `documents/H4_report.md`; old amplification / product-interaction catalogue is **not** listed here). **22 walking bouts**, 11 participants; cycling and tram are not H4 tests. Bout sound = energy-mean A-weighted dBA from 10 s samples; thermal vote at end of bout. Nominal *p*, **no BH**. **Local close:** Spearman ρ(thermal comfort, energy-mean dBA) ρ = −0.62, *p* = 0.002; mixed-linear `thermal_comfort ~ dBA_z + (1|participant)`, *p* = 0.0037. Band-level and L90-excess replacements in `H4_report.md` §2 spectrum. **Not primary H4:** joint `thermal_comfort ~ dBA_z * UTCI_z` (interaction *p* ≈ 0.37; two-route design). Context: coupled thermal×acoustic votes, Cureau vote-on-vote models, route (urban vs green) confound with heat and sound — `H4_report.md` §3. Source equations = cited papers; Brussels results = source-informed adaptations, not exact replications. PRISMA screen: `documents/H4_PRISMA_screening.csv`.
**4.1 Zhen 2025.** *Build Environ* 267:112327. Datang plaza, outdoor UTCI + SPL + TSV. Source: Spearman TSV vs SPL. Local: Spearman thermal comfort vs bout energy-mean dBA (22 walks). Not a product-interaction H4. `Sound_evidence/Sound - Zhen2025 - plaza OTC thermoacoustic Datang.pdf`
**4.2 Cureau 2022.** *Int J Biometeorol* 66:2033–2045. Perugia park walks, wearable LAeq + comfort surveys. Source: Mann–Whitney park vs urban LAeq and votes; Fisher; multinomial overall from ratings. Local: paired Wilcoxon on WalkU−WalkG (sound and thermal votes). Not `thermal_comfort ~ measured dBA`. `H4 - Cureau2022 - multi-domain human-oriented comfort outdoor environments.pdf`
**4.3 Tang 2026.** *Build Environ* 301:114742. Lab playbacks, physiology within thermal strata (HR/SDNN/RMSSD). Source: Kruskal–Wallis across soundscape cells within PET/TSV band. Local: dBA quartiles within median PET on 22 walks; not `thermal_comfort ~ noise`. `Sound_evidence/Sound - Tang2026 - soundscape diversity thermo physiological sex dimorphic.pdf`
**4.4 Peng 2025.** ICUC-12-816. Schiphol courtyards, 24 walkers. Source: five separate one-predictor mixed models (`TSV ~ LAeq`, etc.; equations not printed in abstract). Local: `thermal_comfort ~ dBA_z + (1|participant)` matches source `TSV ~ LAeq` arm. Not `TSV ~ LAeq + UTCI` on one vote. `H4 Sound literature test/NeedScreen - Sound - Peng2025 - Schiphol urban heat noise walking ICUC12 abstract.pdf`
**4.5 Rodrigues 2026.** *J Urban Health* 103:344–356. Lisbon walks, GPS + noise block on physiology (separate from temperature block). Source: `Y ~ z(NOISE_max) + z(NOISE_sd) + u_i`. Local: 10 s dBA max/sd across 22 walks; keep coefficient if nominal *p* < 0.05 and |β| > 0.1. Also H1 ambulatory-design citation. Not thermal-vote ~ noise. `H1 H4 - Rodrigues2026 - urban context emotions bodily responses walking.pdf`
**Excluded (measured heat and/or sound but not updated H4 ports):** Grapas 2026 Brussels (delay ~ exposure, not thermal vote vs dBA; `H4 - Grapas2026 - urban chronotypes climate vulnerability.pdf`); Grapas Rome 2026 (HR/EDA, not thermal-vote H4); Baquero 2019 (stay counts / occupancy; `H4 Sound literature test/NeedScreen - Sound - Baquero2020 - Madrid environmental factors older adults Spanish.pdf`); Chiucchiù 2026 NEXT.ROOM (indoor chamber); Pigliautile 2021; Cureau 2023 (no test); Kraus 2013 (no thermal); Ueberham 2019 (perceived exposure intensity); Korpilo 2024 (restoration); Moshammer 2019 (HRV, weather covariate); Mohammadzadeh, Gao & Zhu, Wang (per `H4_report.md`). Newcastle 2020 and Zouad & Baron 2026 read, not ported. Non-Europe product/2×2 amplification papers: `H4_report.md` §1 only — not in this screened list.

Local: seven additive mixed models (Hamra independent-effects form).

## 6. Shared statistics (H1, H3, H5; centering also H4)
**6.1 Cnaan, Laird & Slasor 1997.** *Stat Med* 16:2349–2380. Mixed model for repeated measures; participant intercept so 10-s rows are not independent. H1, H3. `H1 H3 - Cnaan Laird Slasor 1997 - GLMM unbalanced repeated measures.pdf`
**6.2 Lenth 2023.** emmeans. Planned H1 and H3 contrasts. `H1 H3 - Lenth emmeans - Estimated marginal means.pdf`
**6.3 Benjamini & Hochberg 1995.** *J R Stat Soc B* 57(1):289–300. BH α=0.05 in H1, H2, H3, H5. Not on H4. `H1 H2 H3 H5 - Benjamini Hochberg 1995 - Controlling the FDR.pdf`
**6.4 Enders & Tofighi 2007.** *Psychol Methods* 12(2):121–138. Centering Level-1 predictors. H5 (and H4) exposures participant-centred at phase means or bout-centred at 10 s. `H1 H2 H4 H5 - Enders Tofighi 2007 - Centering predictor variables.pdf`
