# Outdoor field studies (outdoor, wearable/physiological) with small sample sizes

I filtered for **outdoor studies** and avoided indoor-only work as much as possible.

## Candidates you can cite

1) **Acute physiological and perceptual responses to wearing additional clothing while cycling outdoors in a temperate environment: A practical method to increase the heat load** (2017). **N = 7 male cyclists**, cross-over outdoor cycling sessions with repeated measures. Measures included heart rate/thermal load, sweat, thermal sensation and comfort outcomes.
   - DOI: **10.1080/23328940.2017.1365108**  
   - Why useful: true repeated-measures outdoor pilot with very small N, similar mobile context.

2) **Face Temperature as an Indicator of Thermal Stress in Outdoor Work Environments** (Atmosphere, 2020). **12 participants enrolled; 5 usable cases** after strict data quality screening. Outdoor work context with repeated sensing and thermal comfort interpretation.
   - DOI: **10.3390/atmos11060627**  
   - Why useful: accepted example of very small effective sample due field data quality loss.

3) **On-site measurement and mediation analysis of the heat stress and thermal comfort relationship in outdoor microclimates** (2024). **N = 10 (5 female, 5 male)** in outdoor cold-region field routes with repeated thermal/physiological exposure.
   - DOI: **10.1016/j.uclim.2024.102120**  
   - Why useful: directly supports outdoor repeated-observation protocol with N=10.

4) **Dynamic response of pedestrian thermal comfort under outdoor transient conditions** (2019). **N = 24 total (3 participants per walk),** multiple repeated outdoor walk bouts per participant.
   - DOI: **10.1007/s00484-019-01712-2**
   - Why useful: outdoor field design with transient routes and repeated phase-level perception/physiology.

5) **Passive cooling garments for outdoor thermal adaptation** (2024/2025 cohort). **N = 9** in fully within-subject outdoor experiments with physiological + comfort outcomes.
   - DOI: **10.1007/s00484-026-03229-x** (check journal year in your local copy)
   - Why useful: very small N, repeated-measures outdoor design for phase-level inference.

6) **A naturalistic study assessing cyclist physiological stress** (recent, outdoor road context).
   - N = 37 (larger comparator), outdoor naturalistic route with wearable physiological telemetry.
   - Why useful: good high-ecological-validity comparator for method context even though not <10.
