H4 time-series equations — FDR review summary
==============================================

Concise review of FDR results for seven bout-level H4 time-series models (Eq1–Eq7).

FINDINGS
--------
- All seven equations show at least one FDR hit (155 FDR rows total in the main run).
- No FDR on primary exposure terms: UTCI, SND_dBA, or UTCI x sound (no thermal x sound interaction FDR).
- Most FDR is phase contrasts and bout covariates; pupil outcomes carry much of the non-phase FDR (about 6–7 rows per equation).
- Only equations 3 and 4 add heat-term FDRs on pupil via ucm_* heat copies (not on UTCI/dBA/interaction in UTCI equations).

FDR RULE
--------
FDR_significant = TRUE flags a row. Benjamini–Hochberg per term within each equation across 26 planned outcomes (FDR_scope in CSV). Intercepts not FDR-tested.
