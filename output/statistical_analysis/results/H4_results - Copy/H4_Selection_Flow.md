### Parameters and decisions by source

The same reason labels are used throughout. “Derived” describes how a measurement is produced; it is not, by itself, a reason to exclude it.

| Reason label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Aggregation | Raw measurements are replaced by their declared average. |
| Representation choice | A selected formulation, continuous measure, or derived summary is used instead of an alternative or its constituent inputs; “derived” describes if the parameter is produced from other measurements, and is excluded from the parameter list.
| Data availability | Missing observations limit coverage or estimation. |
| Measurement suitability | Definition, calibration or statistical coding needs verification or a different treatment. |
| PCA-informed selection | Inventory 34 sound fields → exclude category text and unidentified proportion → standardize 32 numeric exposure inputs → 10 PCs for B4 (k = max(80% rule, 10); 84.04% cumulative) → 10 original-variable candidates → cumulative sound-only VIF by PC order; full set max VIF 2.11; no VIF removals. |
| Model scope | A valid construct is outside the current equation specification. |
| Variable role | The field is an outcome, participant descriptor, grouping variable, processing field or QC indicator rather than an environmental predictor. |


**1. UCM**

- <u>Retained:</u> AIR RH, AIR temp, HUMIDEX, MRT, PET, SND dBA, SUN Gh, UTCI, WIND AWS.
- <u>AH:</u> Representation choice — AH is absolute humidity (derived/alternate formulation). ucm_AIR_RH is the declared humidity representative.
- <u>AIR T bot, AIR T mid, AIR T top:</u> Representation choice — ucm_AIR_temp is the air-temperature representative.
- <u>GPS speed kmh:</u> Model scope — Movement measures are excluded.
- <u>HUMIDEX SR, MRT S, PET SR, UTCI SR:</u> Representation choice — Derived alternate from Humidex, MRT, PET, UTCI respectively.
- <u>IO flag:</u> Model scope — IO flag is excluded.
- <u>IR back, IR down, IR front, IR left, IR right, IR spot gnd, IR spot left, IR spot right, IR spot sky, IR up:</u> Representation choice — Directional IR channels feed ucm_MRT (CityFeel17).
- <u>OPT:</u> Representation choice — Derived operative temperature from air temperature and MRT (CityFeel17).
- <u>SUN alt, SUN az:</u> Model scope — Solar altitude and azimuth are excluded.
- <u>WIND AWA, WIND TWD:</u> Representation choice — Derived wind direction/angle and True Wind Direction from ucm_WIND_AWS.
- <u>WIND TWS:</u> Representation choice — Derived true-wind speed from ucm_WIND_AWS.
- <u>fp:</u> Model scope — Proximity score (fp) is excluded.

**2. Atmo raw**

- <u>left pm1, left pm10, left pm2.5, right pm1, right pm10, right pm2.5:</u> Aggregation — Replaced by the corresponding left/right PM averages. Raw channels are not entered alongside those averages.
- <u>left humidity, right humidity:</u> Representation choice — UCM ucm_AIR_RH is used instead for its higher temporal resolution.
- <u>left temperature, right temperature:</u> Representation choice — UCM ucm_AIR_temp is used instead for its higher temporal resolution.

**3. Atmo average**

- <u>Retained:</u> pm1 avg, pm10 avg, pm2.5 avg.

**4. LYS1 raw**

- <u>LYS1 kelvin, LYS1 lux:</u> Aggregation — Combined both LYS1 and LYS2 to form kelvin avg and lux avg.
- <u>LYS1 lux cat:</u> Representation choice — Derived illuminance category.
- <u>LYS1 b', LYS1 g', LYS1 r', LYS1 rgbb, LYS1 rgbg, LYS1 rgbir, LYS1 rgbr:</u> Measurement suitability — Exported RGB/IR or r'/g'/b' channel with no declared average or calibrated H4 exposure.
- <u>LYS1 medi:</u> Derived from LYS1 RGB channels, not a direct spectral measurement.
- <u>LYS1 movement:</u> Model scope — LYS on-body movement signal, not an environmental predictor.

**5. LYS2 raw**

- <u>LYS2 kelvin, LYS2 lux:</u> Aggregation — Combined both LYS1 and LYS2 to form kelvin avg and lux avg.
- <u>LYS2 lux cat:</u> Representation choice — Derived illuminance category. Continuous lux is available and no averaged replacement exists.
- <u>LYS2 b', LYS2 g', LYS2 r', LYS2 rgbb, LYS2 rgbg, LYS2 rgbir, LYS2 rgbr:</u> Measurement suitability — Exported RGB/IR or r'/g'/b' channel with no declared average or calibrated H4 exposure.
- <u>LYS2 medi:</u> Derived from LYS2 RGB channels, not a direct spectral measurement.
- <u>LYS2 movement:</u> Model scope — LYS on-body movement signal, not an environmental predictor.

**6. LYS average**

- <u>Retained:</u> kelvin avg, lux avg.
- <u>medi avg:</u> Derived from LYS RGB channels, not a direct spectral measurement.

**7. GVI imagery**

- <u>Retained:</u> GVI 50m.
- <u>GVI grass 50m, GVI phase mean, GVI plant 50m, GVI tree 50m, Grass phase mean, Plant phase mean, Tree phase mean:</u> Representation choice — Alternate GVI scales, class components and phase summaries from the same vision pipeline.

**8. SVF imagery**

- <u>Retained:</u> SVF.
- <u>Building %, Ceiling %, Combined Vegetation %, Grass %, Plant %, Sky %, Tree %, Wall %:</u> Sky openness is SVF. Scene percentages are separate constructs, not equivalent exposures.

**9. Sound**

- <u>SND dBA:</u> Retained in Eq1 and included among 32 numeric exposure inputs. Inventory all 34 sound fields first; exclude category text (nominal) and unidentify_prop (classification diagnostic) before PCA.
- <u>PCA-informed selection:</u> Within-participant-phase centering and unit-SD scaling precede PCA. B4 selection uses 10 PCs (84.04% cumulative variance; the 80% rule alone would retain 9 PCs at 81.15%). A B4-style sequential rule selects 10 distinct original-variable candidates (PC10: `dominant_frequency_hz`).
- <u>VIF screening:</u> All 10 candidates together have sound-only maximum VIF 2.11. Full Eq1 plus these 10 main effects has maximum VIF 2.884–3.092 across 26 outcome-specific designs; all designs are full rank. No terms are removed.
- <u>freq_prop_20_125, vehicle_prop, wind_prop, freq_prop_8000_20000, mechanisms_prop, octave_prop_125_0_hz, urban_manmade_neutral_prop, freq_prop_4000_8000, urban_manmade_positive_prop, dominant_frequency_hz:</u> Additional main effects in proposed Eq2 (sound sensitivity).
- <u>Other numeric sound variables:</u> Not selected by the PCA candidate rule. unidentify_prop is excluded before PCA as a classification diagnostic. These decisions do not establish that omitted variables are scientifically unimportant.
- <u>Interpretation:</u> The 10 original columns do not inherit the PCs' 84.04% variance. This is a proposed sensitivity specification; fitted H4 models have not been updated.

Method, references, selection table and VIF iterations: [Sound_screening_pca_vif.md](<../../../../Presentation material/Sound_screening_pca_vif.md>). PCA references: [King & Jackson (1999)](https://doi.org/10.1002/%28SICI%291099-095X%28199901/02%2910%3A1%3C67%3A%3AAID-ENV336%3E3.0.CO%3B2-0), [Jolliffe & Cadima (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsta.2015.0202). Regression collinearity: [Dormann et al. (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0587.2012.07348.x).

**10. Solar**

- <u>Clearness Index:</u> Derived from Gh/G0 (ClearCI p.3 eq.2).
- <u>Clearness Index G0:</u> Derived from date, time and location only, not personal exposure. Denominator for kt = Gh/G0 (ClearCI p.3 eq.2).
- <u>Clearness Index Infer:</u> Derived categorical clearness from Clearness_Index, not a continuous exposure. No continuous-effect parameterization is declared.

**11. Empatica**

- <u>acc x, acc y, acc z, met, vector magnitude:</u> Model scope — Movement measures are excluded.
- <u>eda phasic, eda tonic, heart rate, hrv fd hf, hrv fd hfnu, hrv fd lf, hrv fd lf hf ratio, hrv fd lfnu, hrv fd total power, hrv fd vlf, hrv td rmssd, hrv td sdnn, skin temperature:</u> Variable role — Used as a physiological or ocular response, not an environmental predictor.
- <u>eda scl usiemens:</u> Used to compute eda phasic and tonic.

**12. Eye-tracker**

- <u>blink duration s, distance from center, eyelid aperture avg, fixation duration s, fixation rate, gaze centrality, gaze dispersion, gaze velocity, pupil change rate, pupil diameter avg, saccade amplitude, saccade duration s, saccade peak velocity:</u> Variable role — Used as a physiological or ocular response, not an environmental predictor.
- <u>stress composite:</u> Derived from other eye-tracker fixation measures.

**13. Questionnaire**

- <u>current comfort 1 7, environmental comfort mean 1 5, pleasantness 1 5, prs11 being away mean 1 5, prs11 coherence mean 1 5, prs11 fascination mean 1 5, prs11 scope mean 1 5, prs11 total mean 1 5, space familiarity 1 5, stai6 mean 1 5, thermal comfort 1 5:</u> Psychological questionnaire outcomes.

**14. Metadata / QC**

- <u>Date, Date (Atmo/LYS), Date (UCM), Date (eye), Datetime, GPS HDOP, GPS alt, GPS hdg, GPS lat, GPS lon, GPS speed valid sample count, GVI match dist m, MAG hdg, ParticipantID, PhaseID, pct complete:</u> Metadata is excluded from the parameter list.
- <u>HRV 5min available, HRV 5min max gap (s), HRV 5min removed pct, HRV 5min segment end, HRV 5min segment start, HRV 5min valid rri count, HRV 5min valid span (s):</u> Metadata is excluded from the parameter list.
- <u>SVF Aggregation Method, SVF Frames In Window, SVF Selection Method, SVF Status:</u> Metadata is excluded from the parameter list.
- <u>age, bike frequency, bmi category who, bmi kg m2, clothing accessories, clothing footwear, clothing height raw, clothing lower body, clothing skin tone, clothing upper body, clothing weight raw, commute modes, daily walking, gender, has children, height m, nationality, own bike for experiment, participant id, regular physical activity, residence type, residential area, response index, site familiarity 1 10, vision correction, weight kg, years in brussels:</u> Metadata is excluded from the parameter list.
- <u>end time s, phase start offset s, segment:</u> Metadata is excluded from the parameter list.

| Eq 6 (PM size sensitivity — PM1): UTCI \* Sound + MRT \* Sound + solar irradiance + SVF (sky view) + PM1 + lux + kelvin (light) + GVI (greenery) | 3.06 |
| Eq 7 (PM size sensitivity — PM10): UTCI \* Sound + MRT \* Sound + solar irradiance + SVF (sky view) + PM10 + lux + kelvin (light) + GVI (greenery) | 3.07 |

Eq2 adds 10 sound main effects; original interactions and phase adjustment remain. The full design has been checked for all 26 outcome-specific samples. `scripts/H4_multivariate.R` defines all seven equations; Step 5 fits Eq2 only when `H4_SPEC$fit_sound_sensitivity` is TRUE (default FALSE). Published fit artifacts remain from the last six-equation run until rerun.
